Cross-venue lock

Rests a YES bid on one venue and a NO bid on the other across reviewed Kalshi ↔ Polymarket pairs. If both fill, the pair pays $1 whatever happens and the profit is fixed at the moment of the fill. It exists to measure the two things that can break that: whether both legs ever fill, and whether the two venues actually resolve the same way.

Ladder basket

Buys NO on every leg of a strike ladder whose YES asks sum above $1 — a payout fixed by contract rather than a bet on any outcome. It exists to test the two things a backtest cannot: whether the legs are genuinely mutually exclusive, and whether all of them actually fill. A basket that only half fills is not an arbitrage, it is a naked position, and that risk is what this measures.

It exists to test the one mechanism that carried 77% of era-1's loss: fills that arrive after the market has already moved against a stale resting bid.
