Kalshi ↔ Polymarket

Logs both venues' books for reviewed pairs of equivalent markets every 5 minutes: the mid gap, and what a maker resting at the bids on both venues would lock after fees (Kalshi 1.75%, Polymarket 0%). Measures whether cross-venue disagreement is frequent and persistent enough to matter — and which venue is the better place to make.
