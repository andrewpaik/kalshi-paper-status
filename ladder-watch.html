About this experiment

From the 2026-08-24 research sweep: NO-baskets on exclusivity-verified ladders at sum(ask)>=1.05 validated at +5.3%/basket [CI +0.5,+11.0] with a contractual floor — but only ~9 baskets/month, and the floor assumes every leg fills as a maker, which no backtest can verify. This logger measures the live stream (frequency, depth, persistence, exclusivity) so a trading mode, if ever built, starts from data instead of hope.

Measurement instrument, not investment advice. This logger never places orders, simulated or otherwise.

Public mirror: each page is regenerated on the source machine every 5 minutes and republished here every 10 minutes, so it can run up to ~12 minutes behind. Code and ledgers are private.
